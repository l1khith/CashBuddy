CashBuddy is a 100% offline, privacy-first personal finance tracker designed for Indian banks and UPI applications. It captures transactions through a three-layer intake system: passive notification parsing, offline screenshot OCR, and fast manual entry.

All financial data remains strictly on your device. The app operates with zero network permissions, zero analytics SDKs, and uses hardware-backed AES-256 SQLCipher encryption.

The F-Droid release uses Tesseract4Android (Apache-2.0) with bundled trained models for 100% local screenshot OCR without any proprietary dependencies.
